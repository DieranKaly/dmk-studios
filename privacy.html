Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy describes how DMK Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use AI Shorts Studio, its desktop application, or this website. The application is designed to process most creation materials locally on your computer.

1. Who is responsible

DMK Studio is the controller for personal information, if any, that we process through this service. For privacy questions or requests, email shunraw@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your computer

The desktop application can create or use local files, including story text and metadata, generated videos and audio, storyboard images, downloaded media and licence records, configuration settings, and YouTube and TikTok OAuth credentials. These are stored in the project’s local folders or locations you configure, such as the output folder, the local database, the `.env` settings file, and the YouTube and TikTok token files. TikTok Client Key and Client Secret values are kept in the local `.env` file. The application does not send these files to DMK Studio.

The application’s activity log is held in its running interface. The application does not currently include an analytics or advertising tracker. Your computer, operating system, security software, and services you choose to connect may have their own logging.

3. Optional connections and information sent to other services

Information leaves your computer when you choose features that need an external service. That service processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. Depending on features you use, this may include:

  • YouTube and Google: YouTube API searches use your configured API key. If you connect YouTube, Google handles authorization. When you upload, the selected video file, title, description, tags, privacy setting, and audience setting are sent to YouTube.
  • Media providers: Searches or downloads you initiate can send search terms or API requests to YouTube, Pexels, Pixabay, or Wikimedia Commons. API credentials you configure are used for their respective services.
  • Model and voice downloads: When you install models or voices, your computer downloads files from their configured provider, such as Hugging Face.
  • Local AI services: Story prompts and narration may be sent to the Ollama server address configured on your computer. By default this is a local server. If you configure a remote server, its operator may receive that information.
  • TikTok: If you connect TikTok, TikTok Login Kit receives the authorization request for your basic profile and posting permission. When you review and confirm a direct post, TikTok receives the selected video file, caption, privacy choice, interaction settings, branded-content choices, and an AI-generated-content indicator. TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored locally. Posting requires TikTok to approve the app’s requested permissions; TikTok controls processing, moderation, and the final post.
  • Instagram: The app opens Instagram’s official website for manual upload. It does not connect to your Instagram account or send videos to Instagram through an API.

Auto run, if enabled, sends each generated video and its upload metadata to the connected YouTube account publicly. It continues on the schedule shown in the app until you stop it or close the desktop application.

4. Website hosting

This policy and the associated information pages are intended to be hosted as static pages using GitHub Pages. The pages do not intentionally add analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information when it hosts or serves the pages. Please see GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its handling of that information.

5. How long information is kept

Files created by the desktop application remain on your computer until you delete them or they are removed by an application cleanup action. You control your local files, settings, and backups. You can remove a saved YouTube token from the project and revoke the application’s access through your Google account settings. Deleting a local copy does not delete content already uploaded to a third-party service; use that service’s controls for uploaded content and account data.

6. Sharing, security, and international processing

DMK Studio does not receive or operate a central account database for the desktop application. When you use an optional connection, the relevant provider receives the information needed for that feature. Providers may process information in other countries under their own terms and safeguards.

Keep your computer secure and do not share your `.env` file, OAuth token, API keys, or private generated materials. No method of local or internet transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your UK data-protection rights

If UK data-protection law applies to our processing of your personal information, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability, or to object to certain processing. You can contact us at shunraw@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Rights may depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

8. Children

The desktop application is a creator tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account system. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through this website, please contact us.
